# And I Wonder — design direction

An open studio for a two-year quant research journey. The home page is three actual architectural openings into a calendar, five research quest lines, and a physical reading library.

Palette: paper #F7F8F2, ink #141414, blueprint #1947E5, cadmium yellow #FFE34A, vermilion #FF5639, mint #B7D6CB. Black 2–3px rules and hard offset shadows describe interactive surfaces. No gradient washes.

Typography: Be Vietnam Pro, supporting both full Vietnamese and English; weight 800–900 display type and 400–600 reading text. Large tightly spaced Swiss headlines, generous reading lines capped at 72 characters. Small drawing dimensions and grid labels only where they explain the calendar or roadmap.

Layout: a compact horizontal studio masthead; giant horizontal triptych of portals takes most of the first screen. Inside each portal is a miniature of its actual destination. Bottom metadata resembles an architectural drawing legend.

Home wireframe: [brand | navigation | VI/EN | owner login] / [large project title + short note] / [DAILY/calendar | PROJECTS/quests | RESOURCES/shelf] / [schedule/date legend].

Daily: generous monthly grid and a compact side panel for today. Projects: five wide quest rows with their actual order, milestone track, and repository name. Resources: frontal bookshelf containing original typographic book covers, rolled research papers, and cassette videos, with useful title tooltips and filters.

Motion: hovering a portal launches 3 subject-specific SVG cutouts along ballistic paths; they settle on the bottom edge, then dissolve into particles after three seconds. Clicking expands that portal through the viewport into the destination. Reduced-motion preference replaces motion with direct navigation. All navigation remains available by keyboard and touch.

Review against the brief: the requested strong outlines, schematic grid, primary colors, block typography, and tactile library justify these choices. Cut unrelated dashboard cards, glowing gradients, and generic decorative labels. Keep the three portals as the memorable moment; reading pages stay calm.
